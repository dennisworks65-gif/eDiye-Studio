Redefining sport for the digital age

Creating a bold identity and e-commerce experience for a performance-driven sports brand, built to inspire athletes and everyday movers alike.

About the project

Parla Porte had a strong product line but a weak brand presence. Their existing identity lacked personality, their e-commerce site felt dated, and their digital experience didn’t match the energy of their products.

We reimagined Parla Porte’s brand from top to bottom, building a visual system that communicates energy, power, and modernity. The new identity uses bold typography, motion-driven graphics, and a color palette that reflects performance and intensity. The e-commerce platform was rebuilt around clean navigation, sharp product storytelling, and high-impact visuals, creating a shopping experience that feels as dynamic as the brand itself.

Challanges

The challenge was building a sports identity that could compete with established giants while feeling authentic and scalable for a growing brand.

Parla Porte needed to move beyond “just another e-commerce shop” and become a recognizable name in the sportswear world. Their old platform lacked visual impact, their brand didn’t have a distinct voice, and their products were getting lost in a crowded market. We had to create a brand system that combined aspirational lifestyle elements with functional product design, and an online store that worked as seamlessly on mobile as it did on desktop.

Summary

Parla Porte emerged with a brand and e-commerce experience that rivals the best in the industry.

With a bold identity, high-performance design system, and an e-commerce platform tailored for conversions, Parla Porte now has the tools to grow into a globally recognized sports brand. The new presence inspires confidence, drives sales, and builds loyalty among athletes and everyday customers alike. It’s not just about selling products — it’s about creating a movement around performance and style.
