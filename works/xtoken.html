About the project

xToken simplifies complex DeFi staking mechanisms into effortless, user-centric investment strategies.

xToken is an innovative decentralized asset management protocol offering seamless staking and liquidity management across modern networks. The core objective was reimagining a traditionally dense, intimidating Web3 trading interface into a streamlined, high-clarity dashboard where users can effortlessly stake tokens, monitor APY yields in real time, and adjust automated liquidity allocations with total confidence.

Challenges & Insights

Bridging institutional-grade transaction accuracy with frictionless consumer usability.

Web3 interfaces frequently struggle with visual noise, opaque gas fees, and bewildering modal confirmations. We conducted user research to distill multi-step protocol actions into modular transaction sheets. By redesigning real-time feedback loops, simplifying wallet interaction states, and establishing a typographic hierarchy with stark dark-mode ergonomics, we eliminated friction across the deposit and withdrawal lifecycle.
