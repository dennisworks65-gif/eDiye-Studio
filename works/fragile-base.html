# E-Commerce # Brand Direction # Digital Flagship # UI/UX

Contemporary fashion house digital commerce experience

Designing a digital flagship for Fragile Base, an avant-garde fashion house built on the intersection of raw architectural strength and emotional vulnerability.

About the fashion house

Fragile Base explores modern tailoring through tactile silhouettes, thoughtful sustainability, and unyielding emotional expression.

Founded on the philosophy that fashion carries responsibility far beyond immediate aesthetics, Fragile Base crafts contemporary garments that embrace paradox: structural durability and intimate delicacy. Our design objective was translating this duality into an immersive online flagship—balancing expansive editorial whitespace with high-converting e-commerce interactions.

Design & Interaction Craft

Crafting tactile online shopping that mirrors the luxury experience of a physical boutique.

High fashion platforms often sacrifice purchasing fluidity for artistic indulgence, or dilute brand identity in generic catalog grids. We developed a bespoke layout architecture: dynamic lookbook pagination, tactile hover previews, a slide-out cart drawer with micro-interactions, and frictionless one-page checkout. Every garment presentation honors fabric drape, weight, and artisan provenance.

Summary

An editorial digital flagship uniting seasonal storytelling with effortless commerce.

The new Fragile Base flagship store captures the house's avant-garde ethos while delivering measurable commercial results: lower bounce rates on editorial pages, seamless mobile purchasing, and an enduring digital presence that establishes Fragile Base among the leaders of contemporary independent fashion.
