Clarity & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Shopify engineering engagements, timelines, and post-launch support.

How long does a bespoke Shopify store build take?

–
A full bespoke Shopify Online Store 2.0 build typically takes 6 to 10 weeks from technical discovery through to cutover. Smaller architectural optimizations or checkout extensibility builds can launch in 3 to 4 weeks. We provide transparent weekly sprint demos and milestone roadmaps from day one.

Do we need Shopify Plus, or is standard Shopify sufficient?

+
Standard Shopify is remarkably capable for brands scaling under $1M/year. Shopify Plus becomes essential when you require Checkout Extensibility, custom backend discounting functions (Shopify Functions), dedicated B2B wholesale portals, multi-store operations, or lower credit card transaction fees. We help you evaluate the exact ROI before making any platform commitment.

Will migrating or rebuilding hurt our existing SEO rankings?

+
No. Preserving your organic search equity is a core technical priority. During Stage 04, we perform a complete URL crawl of your existing store and map 1:1 301 redirects for every product, collection, and blog post. We also preserve structured schema data and page heading hierarchies, ensuring zero traffic dips post-cutover.

Can our creative team manage the store without writing code?

+
100% yes. We build completely within Shopify's native Theme Editor using modular Sections, Blocks, and custom Metaobjects. Your team can spin up landing pages, swap campaign imagery, reorder collections, and update promotional announcements natively with zero ongoing developer dependency.

What kind of support do you provide after launch?

+
We offer ongoing Technical Stewardship Retainers. This includes proactive Core Web Vitals monitoring, app conflict triage, monthly CRO experiments, feature additions, and dedicated war-room coverage during high-volume sales like Black Friday / Cyber Monday.
