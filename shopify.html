FAQ
+

Common questions about working with eDiye Studio.

Everything you need to know about our engagement models, delivery timelines, and ongoing partnership options.

What services does eDiye Studio provide?

–
We specialize in Conversion Rate Optimisation (CRO), end-to-end Design & Development for digital flagships, bespoke Shopify Plus development, UI/UX design systems, custom subscription commerce engines, and ongoing technical stewardship retainers.

How long does a typical design and development project take?

+
A full custom Shopify Online Store 2.0 build typically takes 6 to 10 weeks from technical discovery through to production cutover. Brand identity sprints take 3 to 5 weeks, and focused CRO or checkout extensibility builds can launch in 3 to 4 weeks. We provide transparent weekly sprint demos and milestone roadmaps from day one.

Do you only work with Shopify Plus?

+
Shopify Plus is a core specialty, but we also partner with ambitious standard Shopify and Webflow brands — especially high-growth teams preparing for Plus or those seeking senior craft before they upgrade. We evaluate your exact catalogue and ops complexity to recommend the most cost-effective tier.

How do your ongoing support and growth retainers work?

+
We treat retainers as proactive product ownership rather than an outsourced helpdesk. Our monthly retainers cover continuous Core Web Vitals speed optimization, monthly conversion rate experiments, small feature builds, and dedicated war-room coverage during high-volume sales like Black Friday / Cyber Monday.

How do we begin a project with eDiye?

+
Every engagement starts with a short discovery call where we discuss your business goals, current bottlenecks, and technical requirements. From there, we provide a scoped proposal with transparent milestones, timelines, and team allocation before kickoff.
