Process
+

Our 4-step conversion optimization sprint cycle.

A transparent, recurring framework designed to systematically eliminate drop-off points across your buying funnel.

STEP 01

Audit & Diagnose

We review your analytics, run heatmaps, and analyze user session replays to identify the highest-leverage friction points in your funnel.

STEP 02

Hypothesize & Prototype

We design refined, mobile-first UX solutions and build lightweight variants using native Shopify code with zero third-party script lag.

STEP 03

Split Test & Validate

We deploy the test to a 50/50 live audience split using Intelligems or native server-side routing to measure exact conversion rate and AOV shifts.

STEP 04

Learn, Implement & Scale

Winning variants are merged permanently into your production theme. We document the learnings and immediately queue the next high-impact sprint.

FAQ
+

Common questions about our CRO services.

Everything you need to know about our testing methodology, traffic requirements, and ROI expectations.

How do you come up with tests unique to our store?

–
Every audit begins with your store's proprietary data. We combine Google Analytics 4 drop-off funnels, heatmap session recordings, customer service inquiry logs, and direct user feedback to identify specific friction points unique to your catalog and audience.

What traffic volume do we need to run meaningful CRO tests?

+
For statistically significant A/B testing, we typically recommend a minimum of 30,000 monthly visitors and at least 300 orders per month. For stores below this threshold, we apply high-confidence heuristic UX overhauls and qualitative usability audits that lift conversion without requiring large sample sizes.

How many tests do you run at a time?

+
To maintain clean data and avoid cross-test pollution, we typically run 1 to 2 tests simultaneously across isolated funnel stages (e.g., one on the PDP and one in the cart drawer). This ensures clear attribution for every revenue lift.

Do you also build dedicated landing pages for paid campaigns?

+
Yes. We design and engineer modular, high-speed landing pages tailored specifically for Meta, TikTok, and Google Ads traffic, ensuring message match, frictionless buying, and sub-second mobile loading speeds.

How quickly do we see results from a CRO engagement?

+
Initial heuristic fixes and quick-win optimizations typically ship in Week 2 of our engagement. Live A/B tests generally reach statistical significance within 2 to 4 weeks depending on your store traffic, with compounding monthly gains thereafter.
