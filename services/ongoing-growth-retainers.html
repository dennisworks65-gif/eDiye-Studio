FAQ
+

Common questions about our support retainers.

Everything you need to know about retainer packages, sprint rollover, and team communication.

What's included in an eDiye support retainer?

–
Our retainers cover technical maintenance, speed optimization, new feature builds, conversion experiments, and strategic roadmapping. You get direct access to our senior engineering and design team for both day-to-day tickets and high-level architectural guidance.

How responsive is your team during an emergency?

+
Standard development tasks are triaged and addressed within 24 hours. For critical incidents (such as checkout degradation or checkout gateway errors), we maintain a sub-1-hour emergency response SLA to ensure minimal commercial disruption.

Can we adjust our retainer hours as our business scales?

+
Yes. Our retainers are flexible sprint allocations. As your business enters high-growth phases, seasonal campaign pushes, or major architectural upgrades, we can scale your monthly hours up or down with 30 days notice.

Do unused hours roll over to the next month?

+
We allow a portion of unused hours to roll over into the following month's sprint to give you flexibility around product launches and marketing campaigns, ensuring your retainer investment is never wasted.

Do you only support sites you built originally?

+
Not at all. We frequently take over existing Shopify Plus stores, Next.js web applications, and Webflow sites. We start with a comprehensive technical and code audit to clean up technical debt before transitioning into ongoing sprint stewardship.
